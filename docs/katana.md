# Katana fighter

Catalog ID: katana. Body is below average weight (0.8), diameter multiplier 0.9 (63 at normal diameter 70), 100 starting/reference HP. Weapon has light weight (0.6), curved 130-unit reference blade (117 actual blade units at diameter 63), longer than the current sword and daggers. Initial tuning: damage 8, no damage/movement progression, orbit 8 radians/sec, shared 1-second attack cooldown, no temporary post-hit orbit slowdown.

The special critical effect is bleeding, not a separate immediate damage multiplier. Initial probability is 10%, grows by 2 percentage points after each successful damaging hit, capped at 100%. Roll happens at the pre-growth chance, via the ability's seeded combat RNG. Cooldown contacts, clashes and invulnerable targets do not roll or increase it.

Each critical adds a separate lasting bleeding stack on the victim: 1 HP every game second, starting one second after that critical. Stack timers are independent, never reset each other, and do not expire during the duel. There is no five-second duration. Repeated criticals stack; two stacks cost two HP per second. Bleeding uses normal damage/protection rules: a feeding vampire's invulnerability blocks a scheduled tick without deleting the stack or saving blocked damage for later. Effects are cleared on death and from both participants as soon as a duel ends. A winner cannot keep losing HP after the result.

Crits show a rising gold КРОВЬ notice. A single diagonal wound deepens through four stages on the victim; subsequent criticals keep adding bleeding without further visual growth. Its jagged dark recess, wet red edges and specks stay on the body independently of weapon rotation. This drawing redraws only when stage or body size changes and is preserved in death fragments. Health is displayed below the body with larger numbers. Bleeding stack counts and drop icons are not displayed. Critical hits emit three glossy ballistic blood drops from random points on the cut. Each unblocked bleeding tick emits one more from the wound in a random direction; there is no drip from below the body. These use the shared gravity, wall impacts, splashes and merged surface pools. Tick damage uses the shared floating numbers. Ordinary katana hits retain the shared slash, three ballistic blood droplets, persistent merged surface pools, contact reflection, weapon sparks, weight impacts and wall speed dynamics. Longer weapon fits the death portrait capture and the generic shard-only weapon collider.
