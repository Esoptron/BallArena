# Desperate hunting and Berserk

Vampire: a damaging hit crossing from at least 20 HP to below 20 HP arms a single-use hunt. If a bite starts before activation, the countdown pauses during it and restarts at a full 3 seconds when the bite ends, including an interrupted bite. After 3 game seconds it steers toward the living opponent until a successful bite, with a 20% speed multiplier on top of the normal HP speed curve and a moving wind trail behind its body. During hunting, bite cooldown is cleared and its purple aura disappears. Capture immunity and target protection still apply. Successful biting ends the hunt and restores normal speed/cooldown rules. It cannot activate again in the same duel; reset_runtime restores availability for a new duel. Blocked, zero or lethal damage does not arm it. Healing during the countdown does not cancel the already armed ability. Dead/absent targets and a completed duel cancel hunting. Parameters live in bite.tres: hunt_health_threshold, hunt_delay, hunt_speed_bonus.

Sword: each successful damaging hit has a seeded 5% chance to activate Berserk for five game seconds. Weapon spin is three times normal while active, overriding temporary post-hit orbit slowdown; hit cooldown is 0.5 seconds. The activating hit already uses this cooldown. Further procs refresh five seconds without stacking multipliers. Normal damage and movement progression, reflection, contact latch and body/weapon collision rules stay intact. Timers run while the sword bearer is held, and new battles reset the buff. Berserk adds no immediate damage multiplier. Katana and daggers do not inherit sword's 5% proc.

The proc displays a rising orange БЕРСЕРК notice with a snarling grizzly badge. An upright, gently pulsing grizzly above the swordsman remains visible during the buff; its ring shows remaining duration. It is a small native drawing rather than new particle systems or image processing.
